# NCCN Guidelines Summary: Metastatic Melanoma

## Diagnosis and Workup
- Clinical history and physical examination (including full body skin exam).
- Imaging: CT chest/abdomen/pelvis, MRI brain (if symptomatic or high risk).
- Genetic testing: Mandatory testing for BRAF V600E/K mutations in unresectable or metastatic disease.

## Treatment for BRAF V600E/K Mutated Metastatic Melanoma
- **First-line Therapy:** BRAF/MEK Inhibitor combination therapy (e.g., Dabrafenib + Trametinib).
- **Alternative First-line:** PD-1 blockade (Nivolumab or Pembrolizumab) or Nivolumab/Ipilimumab combination.
- **Dabrafenib + Trametinib Statistics (COMBI-d/v Trials):**
  - Objective Response Rate (ORR): 68%.
  - Complete Response (CR): 19%.
  - 5-Year Overall Survival (OS): 34% overall, rising to 71% in patients achieving a CR.
  - Median Progression-Free Survival (PFS): 11 months.

## Monitoring
- Response assessment every 2-3 months via imaging.
- Dermoscopic monitoring of the primary lesion site if applicable.
